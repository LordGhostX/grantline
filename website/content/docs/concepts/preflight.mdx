---
title: Preflight
description: Preflight checks whether the conditions around an action are safe before execution.
---

Preflight is the set of checks Grantline performs against the state surrounding a proposal before controlled capital can move. It answers whether the action would leave the relevant [Vault](/docs/concepts/vaults) inside the boundaries attached to its [Mandate](/docs/concepts/mandates).

## Before execution

The current Preflight check considers the proposal as a whole and compares its expected native outflow with the Vault's current native balance. It requires the projected balance after execution to stay above the inherited minimum reserve.

When the projected balance is outside its boundary, the result is `DENY` or `ESCALATE` depending on how the Mandate configured that condition. When the boundary holds, the proposal can continue to the other checks.

## What Preflight does not decide

Preflight does not decide whether a strategy is profitable or whether an external asset is eligible. [Guardians](/docs/roadmap) are the planned way to bring external context into an authorisation decision; the current testnet flow has only the native-balance check.

See [Decisions](/docs/concepts/decisions) for how a failed Preflight check affects authorisation and [Mandates](/docs/concepts/mandates) for how conditions attach to authority.
